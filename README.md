### 👋 About Me
I am a 4th-year BS–MS Data Science student at **IISER Thiruvananthapuram**, working at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and medical imaging. I am a researcher at the **Deep Intelligence Learning Lab (DIL LAB)** under the supervision of **Dr. Alwin Poulose**. 

My goal is to build AI systems that are not only powerful but also **interpretable** and **robust** against real-world distribution shifts.

### 🔬 Research Focus
- **Frequency-Aware Deepfake Detection:** Leveraging Fourier and DCT representations to identify artifacts in GAN and Diffusion-generated media.
- **Physics-Guided Medical Imaging:** Developing joint reconstruction and tumor segmentation pipelines for accelerated MRI using Attention U-Nets and $k$-space consistency.
- **Vision Transformers & VLMs:** Exploring hybrid architectures for multimodal reasoning and semantic scene understanding.
- **Trustworthy AI:** Investigating visual robustness, explainability (XAI), and the ethics of technology (Data Colonialism).
